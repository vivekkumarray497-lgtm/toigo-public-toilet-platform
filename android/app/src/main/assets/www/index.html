Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect: TOIGO collects geolocation data only to locate nearby washrooms, and camera feed strictly for live QR code decoding. Camera frames are never stored or transmitted to external servers.

2. Account Data: User profile names, verified emails, and visit timestamps are encrypted and protected with JWT token authentication.

3. Payments: Payment transactions are securely verified server-side. We do not store credit card numbers or UPI PINs.

4. Data Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account and personal data at any time from your Profile screen.

Terms & Conditions

1. Facility Hygiene Standards: All host providers agree to maintain clean washroom standards, running water, soap, and safe access.

2. Pay-Per-Use: Access fees are fixed per washroom visit. Hosts receive 90% direct payout, with 10% platform fee.

3. Conduct: Users must leave verified hygiene ratings honestly and report any safety or cleanliness violations immediately.
